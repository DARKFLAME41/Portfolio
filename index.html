About me

I'm a 3rd year Computer Science student interested in web development and game development.

I like turning ideas into working software — whether that's a platform for students to collaborate, a system for tracking traffic, or a tool for managing court cases. I enjoy the process of breaking a messy problem down into something that actually runs, and then making it a little less messy.

Outside of coursework, I spend a lot of time on personal projects — partly to learn new tools, and partly because I just like building things. I'm especially drawn to projects with a real user in mind, even if that user is just me and my classmates.
